# Second Brain Methodology

The Second Brain in **Personal Life Manager** is structured around the **CODE** framework (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express) adapted for automated and AI-augmented workflows.

---

## 💡 The CODE Framework

### 1. Capture (`crawlers/` & manual inputs)
- Keep only what resonates: insights, high-value articles, bookmarks, and automated summaries.
- Low friction: crawlers ingest content in background queues without interrupting focus.

### 2. Organize (`personal-system/` & taxonomy)
- Designed for actionability.
- Clear separation between long-term reference material, active goals, and recurring routines.

### 3. Distill (`hermes/` LLM pipeline)
- Automated progressive summarization:
  - Level 1: Clean raw markdown.
  - Level 2: Bold key takeaways and metadata.
  - Level 3: Executive synthesis generated by Hermes.

### 4. Express (Outcomes & Life Planning)
- Transforming accumulated knowledge into action: daily routines, project completions, decision logs, and habit reviews.
